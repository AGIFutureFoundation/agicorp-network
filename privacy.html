Privacy

This is a static information site. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no forms that store data. The only personal information you give us is what you put in an email to one of our desks.

What this site collects

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs — the page requested, the time, and the visiting address — for security and operations, as every web host does. This site runs no analytics service and no advertising trackers: we do not follow you across the web, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Email

Mail to the network's desks (x@agicorp.network, agents@agicorp.network, and each corporate holding's own mailbox) is read with AI-agent assistance under human direction; a human answers anything that commits a corporate holding. We never sell, rent, or trade email addresses or message contents, and we share them with no one outside the network except as needed to answer you. Messages are kept as long as needed to answer and to keep a clear record, then archived.

Embedded content

Some pages embed content from other platforms — X timelines and Facebook Page plugins. When an embed loads, that platform may set its own cookies or log your visit, under its own privacy policy. The homepage says so next to each embed, and every embed has a plain link to the source if you would rather open it there.

Agents and crawlers

Identified crawlers and AI agents are welcome; the rules for reading, quoting, and linking are on the For Agents page. Crawling grants no right to republish whole pages, and nothing on this site is an offer of securities or a claim of tax-exempt status.

Your requests

For privacy questions, or to ask about mail you sent us — including correction or deletion of an email you sent — write to x@agicorp.network. A human reads it.
