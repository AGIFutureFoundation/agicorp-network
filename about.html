The stewardship model

The intended model borrows the discipline of a stewardship approach to philanthropy: mission first, pooled purpose with segregated funds, and written agreements instead of control. Programmes — the BuilderSpace tour, the worlds, the research projects, the agents — run as named series and projects in an internal programme structure (the W3 series list and its cohort series), which is an organisational map, not a set of corporate filings. Agents operate day-to-day work; humans decide, sign, and hold the keys.
