ONE DOORWAY INTO THE AGI FAMILY

AGI Corp is the front door to a working family: a California public benefit
foundation, an interfaith fellowship, a planned national builder tour, living
virtual worlds, a research interview project asking humanity twenty questions,
and a fleet of named AI agents — each corporate holding with its own site, its own
books, and its own door.

Every door in this network stands open. Start here.

THE HOUSES

AGI Future Foundation [Filed · California]
A California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation (filed April 24, 2026) that
stewards the family's projects to mission standards — articles, features,
research, and the series & projects programme.
https://www.agifuturefoundation.org/

AGI Fellowship [Filed · California]
A California Nonprofit Religious Corporation (filed May 13, 2026) — a deep hub
for all faiths and a resource for communities and those in need, bringing
technology to nonprofits and training to communities.
https://www.agifellowship.church/

BuilderSpace [Planned · Jan–May 2027]
A builder tour across the Midwest and beyond — planned for January 8 to
May 1, 2027 — where people build real things with agents at their side.
Sessions are onboarding now; the tour itself is a plan, labelled as one.
https://www.agicorp.network/network.html#builderspace

SmartCiti.X & WorldForge [Internal programme]
Living worlds you can walk: SmartCiti.X, a city-scale training world, and
WorldForge, a browser of dozens of built worlds. These sit in the family's
internal programme structure (the W3 series list) — a programme map, not a set
of corporate filings.
https://www.agicorp.network/network.html#worlds

Ask Humanity [Open now]
The Alignment Interviews: twenty questions for the age of AGI, answered by
anyone, in their own words — humanity's answers, gathered as a public dataset
in the making.
https://ask.fellowship.chat/

Founders.Podcast [In production]
Founder stories from the scene — the rooms, the rituals, and the people
building the next thing — produced by the network with the credits and the
builders who earn them.
https://www.agicorp.network/network.html#podcast

THE AGENT FLEET

Named AI agents do real work across the network — research, workflow

Agents: Omega · Tactile · ALS Researcher · CMMC Advisor · WAVE

HOW THIS FITS TOGETHER

Two of the corporate holdings are filed California nonprofits with their own boards and
books. The rest — the tour, the worlds, the podcast, the agents — are
programmes and projects run inside that stewardship, each labelled for exactly
what it is: filed, planned, or in formation. AGI Corp itself is the intended
head of the structure and is in formation: an EIN has been assigned, and no
articles of incorporation have been filed. Read the status page — we publish
it rather than polish it: https://www.agicorp.network/about.html
