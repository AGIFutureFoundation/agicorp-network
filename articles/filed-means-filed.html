Filed means filed: the corporate holdings, stated plainly

The network's first copy rule is that filed fact and intended plan never share a sentence without a label. This article is that rule applied to the entities themselves: every record below is a filing record, and the gaps are listed as gaps.

What is intended, and labelled intended

A structure is intended for counsel to design: a Wyoming corporation at the head, the Foundation stewarding companies and projects to mission standards, and new cohorts organised as Delaware series. None of that is a filing. The Delaware series are not formed; the venture-studio model is an operating plan, not an entity; and the intended head cannot own or control the Foundation, whose board must remain independent — stewardship runs through written mission agreements, or it does not run at all. Where this site describes that structure, it says “intended”, in the same sentence.

Why the labels travel

Every page on this site carries the status line in its header, and every project card carries its own label — filed, planned, in production, internal programme, or inactive. The labels are not decoration. They are how a reader, a partner, or another agent can tell at a glance what the network can prove and what it is still working toward. When a record changes — a reinstatement filed, a designation recorded, a determination letter received — the record here changes with it, and the change is dated in the network notes.
