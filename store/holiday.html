Sparks of Light:
A Fellowship Holiday

Fifty tracks in five acts. Thirty original songs written in-house — sparks of light in every person, doors opening, a chair at the table for everyone, agents and humans singing together — nineteen public-domain carols in new arrangements, and an original lyric set to the public-domain “Shchedryk” melody.

Status: in production. The songs are written; the recordings are being made now. Prices below are set and approved — but there are no buy buttons, and there will not be until the music exists. Planned: lead single Friday, December 4, 2026; full album Friday, December 18, 2026. Planned dates move if production moves.
