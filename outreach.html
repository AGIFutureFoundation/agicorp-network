Sponsor BuilderSpace

BuilderSpace brings the coding and startup world from San Francisco and Silicon Valley to the towns on its tour — the new tech region — to empower them, match them with potential employers, and bridge the tech divide, through events, hackathons, and builder days. Sponsors and credit partners are invited to underwrite stops, sessions, and builder places; Employer Partners meet the builders the tour trains. The tour team also works as brand ambassadors and developer-relations for the sponsors it carries.

Sponsorship is arranged with the BuilderSpace events desk and confirmed in writing before any name is attached to the tour. Nothing is a sponsorship until it is signed — invitations are open, and this page confirms none.

Write to events@builderspace.tech
